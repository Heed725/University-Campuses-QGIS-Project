# Data attribution and map limitations

Geographic data © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. Adapted geographic data retains that licence. See https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/.

Campus outlines are mapped OpenStreetMap features, not surveyed property or ownership boundaries. Chuo Kikuu is an administrative subward and includes UDSM and surrounding areas. Counts represent OSM records, not official asset inventories. Mapping gaps, overlapping footprints and duplicate facility records can exist.

UDSM/Chuo Kikuu features are from the preserved 2 October 2026 snapshot in Heed725/UDSM_Campus_Explorer; boundary features were retrieved 11 October 2026. Ardhi and CoICT extracts and boundaries were retrieved 11 October 2026 from the OSM API.

The projects are independent maps and are not official university publications. Each package includes its own README, attribution, extraction details and limitations.
